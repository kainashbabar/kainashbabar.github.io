Jinlytix Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Jinlytix is a private, personal analytics tool used only by its owner to view analytics for the owner's own YouTube channels.

  • It requests read-only access to YouTube and YouTube Analytics data (youtube.readonly, yt-analytics.readonly, yt-analytics-monetary.readonly).
  • Data and access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, encrypted. Nothing is sent to any third-party server, sold or shared.
  • It cannot upload, edit or delete anything on YouTube.
  • Jinlytix's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing a channel in Jinlytix deletes its locally stored token and data.
